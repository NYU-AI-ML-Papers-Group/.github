# NYU AI-ML Papers Group

A weekly working group of NYU researchers and doctoral students reading, critiquing, and writing research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and behavioral and mental health.

## About the group

The Papers Group is the internal research team behind a growing body of work on AI in behavioral health: multi-agent systems, just-in-time adaptive interventions (JITAI), computational phenotyping, and AI-assisted qualitative analysis. Members meet to discuss new papers in the field and to advance the group's own manuscripts from first draft to submission. The group is organized by Besa Bauta, Keng-Yen Huang, and Hyungrok Do.

## Meetings

- **Tuesdays, 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM ET**, on Zoom.
- Each session combines a paper discussion with workstream updates and a check-in on the group's manuscripts.

## Current workstreams

- **Multi-agent systems literature review** (lead: Justin Liu): an agent taxonomy for behavioral health applications, connecting to the group's phenotyping and RDoC papers.
- **LLM qualitative analysis** (leads: Hana Bauta and Asma Darwish): testing AI as an inter-rater reliability agent across interview transcripts, validated on team data and then on partner data from Kaohsiung Medical University.
- **Workforce prediction** (with Eric Chou): modeling stress, depression, and dropout risk from a longitudinal workforce dataset from Uganda (three time points, about 600 cases).
- **ICOBM conference presentation paper** (lead: Eric Chou): the group's Kaohsiung Medical University collaboration paper.
